## Landing, chat and admin

- **Scope and modes:** Citizen.ng landing page (`/`, Persuade), public document-grounded chat (`/chat`, Operate), and allowlisted team content shell (`/admin/*`, Operate).
- **Audience and job:** Nigerian citizens arrive with a civic question but may not know which document or institution to consult. The Citizen team manages the approved document library and mini app content.
- **Action and evidence:** The main action is to ask a question. The product promise is traceable answers linked to retrieved public documents. No Citizen corpus has been supplied, so do not invent quotations, live results, or indexed-document counts.
- **Direction:** Use America.gov as the structural reference: dark welcome, oversized centered editorial serif greeting, a wide six-image slideshow of Nigerian landscapes and public spaces paired to changing example questions, and one prominent prompt surface. Put a small Nigerian flag beside the header wordmark. Keep this serif in the hero and its wordmark only. Inter sets every lower section and work surface. Use near-black, deep forest green, and warm cream. Below the hero, use a large sans statement, a document illustration, question routes, and an election-led mini app preview with three original photographs and dark text-protecting gradients. The generated images are atmospheric, not evidence of real events. Chat follows America.gov/chat's narrow reading column, right-aligned user question, plain answer text, and anchored dark composer. Do not use visible divider or separator lines anywhere; rely on whitespace and changes in surface color. Keep the Citizen brand as text without a separate icon.
- **Signature interaction:** Ask from the home prompt or a suggested question, then inspect citations linked to the underlying passage.
- **Boundaries:** Keep chat restricted to the curated library; do not add open web search, news, or document-independent factual answers. Admin APIs enforce Google email allowlisting. Mini apps remain separately maintained and deploy beneath their own path prefixes.
- **Open decisions:** Document storage and formats, embedding provider, indexing and version policy, deployment path routing, and public chat history/retention.
